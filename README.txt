FRONTS & RIVALS 2.0
===================

A free single-player script mod for Grand Theft Auto V Enhanced (Story Mode).
Not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games.

Take real Los Santos businesses from rival crews, post guards with names, faces, skills
and ranks, and hold them against three rival crews who already own most of the
city and fight each other for the rest. Attacks play out on their own while
you're away and turn into real fights when you're close. Michael, Franklin and
Trevor share one empire, and it runs alongside the whole story.


REQUIREMENTS
------------
Install these yourself first. The mod never bundles them.

  GTA V Enhanced, Story Mode
      Steam, Epic or the Rockstar Games Launcher

  BattlEye off (script mods can't load with it on)
      Rockstar Games Launcher > Settings > GTA V Enhanced,
      or add -nobattleye to the launch options

  Script Hook V (loads script mods into the game)
      http://www.dev-c.com/gtav/scripthookv/

  Script Hook V .NET Enhanced (runs .NET script mods like this one)
      https://github.com/Chiheb-Bacha/ScriptHookVDotNetEnhanced/releases

  LemonUI, the SHVDN3 build, 2.2 or later (the game-style menus)
      https://github.com/LemonUIbyLemon/LemonUI/releases

  iFruitAddon2 3.x (the Office contact on your phone)
      https://github.com/Bob74/iFruitAddon2/releases
      Optional: without it, press RB + D-pad Left or O to open the Office

  .NET Framework 4.8 (needed by Script Hook V .NET; already on most PCs)
      https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet-framework/net48

  Visual C++ Redistributable, x64, latest (needed by Script Hook V .NET;
  already on most PCs)
      https://learn.microsoft.com/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist

Tested with Script Hook V v3889.0, Script Hook V .NET Enhanced 1.1.0.6,
LemonUI 2.2 and iFruitAddon2 3.1.1. After a GTA V update, wait for Script Hook V
to update before playing or reporting bugs.

Playing on the older Legacy edition? It should work but hasn't been tested there
yet. Use the Script Hook V .NET nightly build instead (3.6.0-nightly.89 or later):
      https://github.com/scripthookvdotnet/scripthookvdotnet-nightly/releases


INSTALL
-------
As with any mod, back up your saves first: copy your GTA V save folder
(Documents/Rockstar Games/GTAV Enhanced/Profiles) somewhere safe before
installing. Fronts & Rivals never touches GTA's own saves, but a backup costs
nothing.

1. Install everything in Requirements
2. Copy the contents of this zip's "scripts" folder into <GTA V>\scripts\ :
   FrontsAndRivals.dll, FrontsAndRivals.ini and the FrontsAndRivals folder
3. Start Story Mode. After Franklin's first mission, a help box points you at a
   car wash the Dead Kings hold

Windows may need to let mods create files in the GTA V folder. The mod saves to
scripts\FrontsAndRivals\empire.json.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
  Open the Office      Press RB + D-pad Left on a controller or O on
                       keyboard, or call The Office on your phone
  First front          Go to the Hands On Car Wash in Strawberry, take out
                       the three Dead Kings watching it (pistols and bats)
                       and it's yours; then hire recruits in the Office
                       and post them there
  Next Step            The line at the top of the Office always says what to
                       do next
  Guards               Hire and post them from the Office, then arm them and
                       give them body armour
  Midnight             Every front pays out at in-game midnight, after wages
  Defend               When a front is hit, a timer shows how long your guards
                       can hold. Race there and fight beside them, or leave it
                       to them
  Take rival fronts    Take out every soldier at a crew's front and it's
                       yours on the spot, or send a crew from the Office


WHAT'S IN 2.0
-------------

  FRONTS
 - 29 real businesses across Los Santos: bars, clubs, liquor stores, motels,
   diners, markets, factories and garages
 - Every front starts in a crew's hands and nothing is for sale: you take
   them by force, in person or with a raid
 - Bigger fronts pay more and fight harder: 3 kids with pistols and bats at
   the car wash, 3 soldiers at other small fronts, 5 at mid-size fronts and 8
   at the biggest, with better guns and armour as they grow
 - Taking a front plays out like finishing a job in Story Mode: the gold
   shard, the chime and a shot of the building
 - Every front pays out at midnight, about what Story Mode properties pay,
   and more the longer you hold it and the more you own
 - Guard posts and upgrades by size: small fronts take 2 guards, mid-size 3
   and the biggest 4, with Cameras, Floodlights, Reinforced Doors and an
   Armory
 - Staff at the front you're near: a cashier on the phone, a janitor
   sweeping, a chef on a smoke break

  YOUR CREW
 - Guards with skills like the player's (Shooting, Strength, Stamina and
   Stealth) that grow on the job, with ranks, traits and a memorial for the
   fallen
 - A weapon ladder: every guard starts with a knife and is armed one gun at a
   time at Ammu-Nation's prices, and better guns unlock with rank
 - Body armour that stays on: guards heal and re-vest after every fight
 - Bodyguards you earn: your first at 8 fronts and a second at 14, Soldiers
   or better only, who follow you and fight at your side
 - Raids: send up to four reserve guards to take a rival front without you,
   with the odds shown first
 - Your colours: your guards wear your crew colour in the style you pick
   (Street, Smart, Biker or Mixed)

  RIVAL CREWS
 - Three original crews on their own turf: the Dead Kings in South LS,
   Hellfire MC on the east side and the Vinewood Elite out west, each with a
   boss and three lieutenants
 - Every crew member wears their crew colour on a major piece of clothing, so
   you always know who you're fighting
 - Crews remember who hurts them: kill their people on the street, yourself
   or through your guards, and that crew gets angrier, from Calm to Vendetta
   (shown on its Rivals page)
 - A nemesis loop: leaders who escape come back with scars and text you about
   it, and dead lieutenants are replaced by promoted survivors
 - A city that moves without you: once you've taken your first front, the
   crews feud and take each other's fronts at night

  SOLDIERS ON ALERT
 - Walk up on foot and rival soldiers watch you, then raise their guns and
   warn you off, and they open fire if you stay, walk right up, aim at one or
   shoot
 - Back off and they go back to work
 - Tempers vary: small fronts warn longest, hot streets and angry crews
   barely warn, and Hellfire MC have the shortest fuse
 - Sneak up if you'd rather: they only notice what they can see, crouching
   and darkness let you get much closer, and silenced shots are only heard up
   close
 - Street crews hanging out on their turf warn you off the same way before
   they fight

  STREET LIFE
 - Crews on their streets, like Saints Row: they hang out, walk the block or
   cruise their turf in their colours, and your own crew does the same around
   your fronts
 - A rougher city: now and then crew members mug people, carjack drivers, get
   chased by the police or bundle someone into a car, and rival crews shoot
   it out on turf borders
 - A big, mixed cast of ordinary Los Santos street people, women and men,
   with nicknames for only some of them

  FIGHTS
 - Attacks with no warning: one alert, a timer showing how long your guards
   can hold (about 5 minutes, more with Cameras), a GPS route and a bonus for
   holding the door yourself
 - Live defenses within 300 m: crews arrive in convoys in their own vehicles
   (Hellfire MC on motorcycles), with their own guns and the odd explosive
 - Everyone in a fight, yours and theirs, has twice an ordinary person's
   health, so fights last
 - Running soldiers over counts, but the whole front opens fire on your car
   and calls for backup
 - Street heat, shown in the Office: every front you take raises it and every
   night cools it, and the hotter it is, the sooner the crews hit back, so
   lying low for a night or two pays off
 - A text tells you when the streets heat up or cool down, and when a crew
   gets furious or calms down

  MADE FOR STORY MODE
 - Progression money can't skip: guns unlock with rank, bigger fronts need a
   bigger empire (3 and 8 fronts held) and new fronts get two quiet days
 - Difficulty from Relaxed to Brutal, Reset All Fronts to take the city again
   with the crew you built, and Start a New Empire for a new story game
 - Settings > How It Works explains every system in the game, and first-time
   tips show in GTA's help box
 - The game's own sounds, a message queue that never spams and full
   controller support
 - Nothing to collect and no upkeep chores: good guards win without you

PLAYING ALONGSIDE THE STORY
---------------------------
Fronts & Rivals is built to run for your whole Story Mode playthrough.
Everything pauses during missions, cutscenes, random events and character
switches, and attacks only count free-roam time, so a long mission never costs
you a front.

Money is at Story Mode scale, and money can't buy you fronts: every one is
taken by force. Early Franklin can take the car wash with a pistol; bigger
fronts need guards, guns and a bigger empire. A steady player holds about 8
fronts after 10-12 hours, 15 after about 25 and 22 after about 40, roughly the
length of the story; even someone fighting non-stop can't take the city in an
evening.


SETTINGS
--------
The everyday ones are in the game under The Office > Settings: notifications,
sound effects, GPS routes to attacks, map blips, difficulty, attack frequency,
rival turf moves, live defenses and permanent death.

Everything else is in scripts\FrontsAndRivals.ini, with each line explained in
the file (attack timing, notifications, income and wage multipliers, raids,
bodyguards, map icons and more).


COMPATIBILITY
-------------
 - Single-player only; the mod switches itself off if a GTA Online session
   starts
 - It never changes story missions, characters or GTA V's own save files
 - It removes its people during missions, so missions never see them
 - At most 28 people and 8 vehicles at once, all cleaned up when you leave,
   reload scripts or start a mission
 - Job and money mods should be fine; they just give you more cash (lower
   IncomeMultiplier in the INI to compensate)
 - Don't run it alongside other gang or turf-war overhauls; both would compete
   for the same people and gangs


UNINSTALL
---------
Delete FrontsAndRivals.dll, FrontsAndRivals.ini and the scripts\FrontsAndRivals
folder. Cash the mod paid out stays with your characters.


KNOWN ISSUES
------------
 - The Office contact sits at the end of the phone's contact list; that's where
   iFruitAddon2 puts mod contacts so story contacts are never overwritten
 - A few fronts have fewer than four guard posts where the street layout leaves
   no safe spot
