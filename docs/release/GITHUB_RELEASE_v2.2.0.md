# Fronts & Rivals 2.2.0

A bug-fix update from player reports. Your empire carries over.

## Fixes

- Every front now ships with its guard spots already placed and checked in the game: clear of walls and ledges, on the door's level and spread out, so soldiers stand where they should from the first visit
- Extra soldiers at a big front spread out instead of standing on top of each other
- Guards and crew members are all fit young adults: old, overweight and homeless-looking people are gone from the lists, and older saves swap them out by themselves
- Every front gets guards: where the game gives no walkable spots by the door (some liquor stores and the Eclipse Lounge), the soldiers now stand on the ground around it instead of never turning up
- A crew worn down by your attacks still guards every front it holds, so its last fronts can always be cleared and taken
- Guard spots found near one front no longer reset a fight at another front
- The takeover camera shot is skipped while your phone is out, so the phone no longer fills the screen
- A raid rolls the odds you were shown when you sent it, even if night falls while your people are on the way
- A raid whose front changed hands before your people got there is called off, not failed, and doesn't use up the day's raid

Balance is unchanged: in 300 simulated games a steady player still reaches 8, 15 and 22 fronts by about day 9, 24 and 38, and holds 74% of attacks.

## Download

`FrontsAndRivals-2.2.0.zip` below. Copy the contents of its scripts folder into your GTA V scripts folder, replacing the old files.

Requires Script Hook V, Script Hook V .NET Enhanced and LemonUI (SHVDN3 build). iFruitAddon2 is optional. Single-player only, and free.
