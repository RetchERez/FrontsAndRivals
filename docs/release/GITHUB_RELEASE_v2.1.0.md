# Fronts & Rivals 2.1.0

A bug-fix update from player reports. Your empire carries over.

## Fixes

- Every rival front is guarded: a crew short of people still keeps a skeleton guard on each front it holds (2 at a small front, 3 mid-size, 4 big), so no front is ever empty and free for the taking
- A front you clear is yours even if the fight pulled you away from its door, as long as you or your people dropped one of its soldiers
- A front always counts as cleared once every soldier who turned up is down, even if one never managed to appear
- Crew dots on the map disappear when the soldier or street crew member they belong to is killed
- No more guards with floating hands and missing arms: if a top or its arms can't be shown in your copy of the game, the guard wears another whole outfit in your colour instead

Balance is unchanged: in 300 simulated games a steady player still reaches 8, 15 and 22 fronts by about day 9, 24 and 39, and holds 74% of attacks.

## Download

**FrontsAndRivals-2.1.0.zip** below. Copy the contents of its scripts folder into your GTA V scripts folder, replacing the old files.

Requires Script Hook V, Script Hook V .NET Enhanced and LemonUI (SHVDN3 build). iFruitAddon2 is optional. Single-player only, and free.
